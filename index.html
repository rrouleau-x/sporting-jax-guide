Age-Appropriate Player Guidance

Tournament weekends are exciting and demanding for young players. Here are some practical reminders.

  • Prioritize sleep — early bedtimes Friday and Saturday
  • Keep meals familiar — tournament weekend is not the time for new foods
  • Bring simple, preferred snacks
  • Encourage regular hydration throughout the day
  • Limit exhausting hotel-pool activity before games
  • Bring quiet activities for downtime between games
  • Expect physical and emotional fatigue — especially after a loss
  • Keep postgame conversations positive and brief
  • Allow coaches to provide soccer instruction
  • Emphasize effort, teamwork, learning, and enjoyment

Tournament Rules Parents Need to Know

Official Tournament Information. Full rules: 2026 Sporting Jax Official Rules

PointsWin = 3 • Tie = 1 • Loss = 0
Game length25-minute halves for U9-U12
Mercy ruleMatch ends when a team leads by 6 at halftime or later
SubstitutionsUnlimited — goal kicks, throw-ins, kickoffs, halftime, injury
Zero toleranceNo referee abuse. Immediate removal + minimum 1-game suspension
HeadingNot allowed for U12 and younger
Water breaksMandatory mid-half at 85°F+ — expected all weekend
Forfeit5 minutes late from scheduled kickoff. Min 5 players to start
Weather30-minute delay clock. Post-halftime stoppage = completed game
ChampionshipNo overtime — straight to penalty kicks if tied

Tiebreakers (in order)

1Head-to-HeadResult between tied teams (disregarded if 3+ teams tied)
2Goal DifferentialGF minus GA. Max 4 goals per game.
3Goals ForTotal scored. Max 4 goals per game.
4Goals AgainstFewest allowed (no cap)
5ShutoutsMost clean sheets
6Penalty KicksFIFA Kicks from the Penalty Mark

The 4-goal cap on Goal Differential and Goals For is the most commonly misunderstood rule. The cap applies per game, not per tournament.
