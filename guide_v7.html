Tournament Rules Parents Need to Know

Official Tournament Information. Full rules: Official Rules Document →

Format7v7 • Max roster 12 • Min 5 to start • Size 4 ball
Game length25-min halves • 5-min halftime
PointsWin = 3 • Tie = 1 • Loss = 0
Mercy rule6-goal lead at halftime or later = match ends
Goal capMax 4 goals per game for GD and GF tiebreakers
SubstitutionsUnlimited at goal kicks, throw-ins, kickoffs, halftime, injury
HeadingNot allowed U12 and younger
Water breaksMandatory mid-half at 85F+ (FYSA Rule 402.4)
Forfeit5 min late (10 min finals). Min 5 players. 4-0 loss.
Zero toleranceNo ref abuse — immediate removal + suspension
Weather30-min delay clock. Post-halftime stoppage = completed game
ChampionshipNo OT — straight to PKs if tied

Tiebreakers (in order)

1Head-to-HeadDisregarded if 3+ teams tied
2Goal DifferentialMax 4 per game
3Goals ForMax 4 per game
4Goals AgainstFewest allowed (no cap)
5ShutoutsMost clean sheets
6Penalty KicksFIFA Kicks from the Penalty Mark
